How to play
  • On your turn, pick one of your pits (bottom row). Its stones are sown one by one, counter-clockwise, starting in the next pit.
  • Your store is on the right. Stones go into it as you pass, but never into your opponent's store.
  • End in your own store and you move again.
  • End in an empty pit on your side and you capture that stone plus everything in the pit opposite.
  • When one side runs out of stones, the other side keeps its remaining stones. Most stones in the store wins.
  • Keys 1–6 play the pits from left to right.
