How to play

  1. Each player gets 10 cards. One card is turned up to start the discard pile and the rest are the stock.
  2. Melds are sets of 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, or runs of 3 or more in one suit (Ace is low: A-2-3, not Q-K-A). Cards not in a meld are deadwood: face cards count 10, Aces 1, others their number. Your hand is arranged into its best melds for you.
  3. First turn: the non-dealer may take the upcard or pass. If they pass, the dealer may take it or pass. If both pass, the non-dealer draws from the stock.
  4. On each turn, draw the top card of the stock or the discard pile, then discard one card. You can't throw back the card you just took from the discard pile.
  5. Knock when your deadwood after discarding is 10 or less. The hand ends, and your opponent may lay off their deadwood onto your melds.
  6. If your deadwood is lower, you score the difference. Knock with no deadwood at all for gin: 25 bonus points plus your opponent's deadwood, and no lay-offs.
  7. Undercut: if the defender's deadwood is equal to or less than the knocker's, the defender scores 25 plus the difference.
  8. If only 2 cards are left in the stock and nobody has knocked, the hand is dead and is redealt. The winner of each hand deals the next.
  9. The first player to reach the target wins and adds a 100-point game bonus plus 25 for every hand they won. A shutout (opponent won no hands) doubles the winner's total.
