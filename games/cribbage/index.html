How to play

  1. Each player gets 6 cards and puts 2 face down into the crib. The crib belongs to the dealer, and the deal alternates every hand.
  2. The top card of the deck is turned up as the starter. If it's a Jack, the dealer pegs 2 (his heels).
  3. The play: starting with the non-dealer, take turns laying a card and calling the running total, which can't go past 31. Face cards count 10 and Aces 1.
  4. During the play you score for making the count 15 (2) or 31 (2), for a pair with the last card (2; three of a kind 6, four 12), and for a run of 3 or more among the latest cards, in any order (1 per card).
  5. If you can't play without passing 31, you say go. Your opponent keeps playing if they can, and whoever plays the last card before the count restarts pegs 1 (2 for exactly 31). The last card of the hand also scores 1.
  6. The show: each hand counts with the starter: every combination adding to 15 scores 2, each pair 2, each run 1 per card, four cards of one suit 4 (5 with the starter), and the Jack matching the starter's suit 1 (nobs). The non-dealer counts first, then the dealer, then the dealer's crib. A crib flush needs all five cards.
  7. The first player to reach 121 (or 61 in a short game) wins, even in the middle of a hand. Win before your opponent passes 90 and it's a skunk.
