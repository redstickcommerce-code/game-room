How to play

  1. Each player starts with 7 tiles from a double-six set. The rest are the boneyard. The highest double leads the first hand, and after that the winner of the last hand leads any tile.
  2. On your turn, play a tile whose number matches an open end. If you can't, draw from the boneyard until you can. If the boneyard is empty, pass.
  3. Score by fives. After each play, add up the open ends. If the total is a multiple of 5, you score that many points.
  4. A double at an open end counts both of its halves.
  5. The first double played is the spinner. Once both of its long sides have been played, its other two sides open up too. Those sides count only after a tile is played on them.
  6. Play your last tile to win the hand and score your opponent's remaining pips, rounded to the nearest 5.
  7. If nobody can play, the hand is blocked. The lower pip total scores the difference, rounded to the nearest 5.
  8. The first player to reach the target wins, even in the middle of a hand.
