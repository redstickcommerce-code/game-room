SLIDE & BUMP

New game

Draw a card, move a pawn, and get all four of yours home first.

Players

Who's playing

Computer difficulty

How to play

  1. Everyone has four pawns waiting in Start. Pawns travel clockwise around the board, then turn into their own coloured safety lane and finish in Home.
  2. On your turn, draw a card and use it to move one pawn. If the card gives you no legal move, you lose the turn.
  3. 1 or 2: bring a pawn out of Start, or move forward that many. A 2 also lets you draw again.
  4. 4 moves a pawn backward 4. Backing up from just outside Start is a shortcut toward Home!
  5. 7: move 7, or split the 7 between two pawns. 10: forward 10 or back 1. 11: forward 11, or swap places with any opponent's pawn on the main track.
  6. 3, 5, 8, 12: move forward that many.
  7. BUMP! card: take a pawn from your Start and jump onto any opponent's pawn on the main track, sending it back to its Start.
  8. Land on an opponent's pawn and it goes back to Start. You can't land on your own pawn.
  9. Slides: land exactly on the arrow at the start of a slide that isn't your colour and you zoom to its end, bumping every pawn on the slide back to Start (even your own).
  10. Your safety lane is yours alone, so pawns in it can't be bumped. You need the exact count to land in Home.
  11. The first player with all four pawns Home wins.
