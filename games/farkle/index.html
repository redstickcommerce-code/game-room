How to play

  1. Tap Roll. Tap the scoring dice you want to keep. Every die you select has to score.
  2. Roll the rest to push your luck, or Bank the turn total into your score.
  3. If a roll has no scoring dice, that's a Farkle: you lose everything from this turn.
  4. Hot dice: score all six and you get all six back to keep rolling.
  5. Combinations only count within a single roll. A 1 kept earlier can't join a pair of 1s rolled later.
  6. You need the opening minimum in one turn before your first bank counts.
  7. When someone reaches the target, everyone else gets one last turn to beat them.
