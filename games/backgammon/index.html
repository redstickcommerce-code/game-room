How to play

  1. Race all 15 of your checkers around the board into your home (the six points at the bottom right for White), then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.
  2. Roll two dice. Each die is a separate move. Doubles play four times.
  3. Tap a checker, then tap one of the glowing points to move it. You can't land on a point holding two or more of your opponent's checkers.
  4. Land on a lone opposing checker (a blot) to hit it onto the bar. A checker on the bar must come back in through the opponent's home before anything else can move.
  5. You must use both dice if you can. If only one can be played, you must play the larger one if possible.
  6. Once all your checkers are home, move them onto the tray to bear off. A die bigger than needed can bear off your farthest checker.
  7. Win before your opponent bears any off for a gammon (2 points). If they also still have a checker on the bar or in your home, it's a backgammon (3 points).
