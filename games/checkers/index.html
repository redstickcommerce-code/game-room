How to play

  1. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto a dark square.
  2. Jump over an opponent's piece into the empty square beyond to capture it. If another jump is available from there, keep going.
  3. Captures are required. If you can jump, you must. Pieces that can capture glow when you have to.
  4. Reach the far row and your piece is crowned a King. Kings move and jump backward too.
  5. Win by capturing every opposing piece or leaving your opponent with no legal move.
  6. 40 moves each with no capture and no plain piece moving is a draw.
