How to play

  1. Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play one card that matches the top card's colour or its number or symbol.
  2. If you can't or don't want to play, draw one card. If it can be played you may play it right away; otherwise your turn ends.
  3. ⊘ Skip: the next player misses a turn. ⇄ Reverse: play changes direction (with two players it works like Skip). +2: the next player draws 2 and misses a turn.
  4. Wild: play it on anything and choose the colour. Wild +4: choose the colour, and the next player draws 4 and misses a turn. You may only play a Wild +4 when you have no card of the current colour.
  5. When you play your second-to-last card, tap Last card! first. Forget, and you draw 2 as a penalty.
  6. The first player to empty their hand wins the round and scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: numbers at face value, Skip, Reverse and +2 at 20, Wilds at 50.
  7. Play one round, or keep dealing until someone reaches the target score.
