How to play

  1. On your turn, tap between two neighbouring dots to draw a line.
  2. Draw the fourth side of a box and it's yours. You then have to draw another line.
  3. When every line is drawn, whoever owns the most boxes wins.
  4. Tip: try not to draw the third side of a box. That hands it to your opponent, and often a whole chain of boxes with it.
