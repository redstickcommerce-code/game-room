YACHT

New game

Five dice, three rolls a turn, thirteen boxes to fill.

Players

Who's playing

Computer difficulty

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll the five dice up to three times. After each roll, tap any dice you want to hold; the rest are rolled again.
  2. Then pick one empty box on your scorecard and score the dice there (tap it, then tap again to confirm). You must fill a box every turn, even if it scores 0.
  3. Ones to Sixes score the total of that number. If those six boxes add up to 63 or more, you get a 35 bonus.
  4. Three / Four of a kind: the total of all five dice. Full house (three of one, two of another): 25. Small straight (four in a row): 30. Large straight (five in a row): 40. Chance: the total of any dice.
  5. Yacht (all five the same): 50. Each extra Yacht after that earns a 100 bonus, as long as your Yacht box scored 50.
  6. Joker rule: for an extra Yacht, use the matching Ones–Sixes box if it's empty. If that's filled, any lower box scores in full (a Full house counts 25, straights 30 and 40). If those are all full too, take a 0 in an upper box.
  7. After 13 rounds the highest total wins.
