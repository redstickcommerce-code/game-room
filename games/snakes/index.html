SNAKES & LADDERS

New game

Race from 1 to 100. Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes.

Players

Who's playing

Reaching 100

Rolling a 6

How to play

  1. Everyone starts off the board. On your turn, roll the die and move your token that many squares, following the numbers from 1 up to 100.
  2. Land at the foot of a ladder and you climb straight up to its top.
  3. Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.
  4. Any number of tokens can share a square.
  5. The first to reach 100 wins. Depending on the setting, you either bounce back by the extra squares if you roll too high, need the exact roll (otherwise you stay put), or win with any roll that gets you there.
  6. With Roll again on, a 6 earns another turn.
