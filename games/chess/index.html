How to play

  1. Tap a piece, then tap a marked square to move it. A dot is an empty square, and a ring is a capture.
  2. Put the enemy king in checkmate to win: it is under attack and nothing can stop it.
  3. Castling: tap your king, then the square two to its left or right. Neither piece can have moved, and the king can't castle out of, through or into check.
  4. En passant: if a pawn jumps two squares and lands beside your pawn, you can capture it as if it had moved one, on the very next move only.
  5. Promotion: a pawn reaching the far row becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight of your choice.
  6. The game is a draw by stalemate, threefold repetition, 50 moves without a capture or pawn move, or when neither side has enough pieces to mate.
